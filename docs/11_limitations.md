# Limitations

- Historical anonymized dataset; no current-market claims.
- GMV proxy only; platform revenue, margin, CAC, and ROAS are unavailable.
- Olist marketing funnel is sampled; not every lead links to a marketplace seller.
- Observational associations are not causal effects.
- Multi-seller orders complicate seller-level delivery/review attribution.
- Recent retention cohorts are censored; ineligible M6 cells are null.
- Geography uses postcode/state approximations.
